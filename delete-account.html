BoxSorter - Delete your data

by DnhFun

BoxSorter has no login. The only information that leaves your phone is what the online ranking needs: your nickname, your country (two letters), your scores and times, which achievements you have, and a random ID made for the ranking (an anonymous account). You can delete all of it at any time.

In the game (right away)

  1. Open BoxSorter and tap Settings (the gear).
  2. Tap your name (the first line).
  3. Tap Delete my online data, then YES, DELETE.

All your ranking entries, your achievements list and the anonymous account are deleted at once. After that the game sends nothing, unless you tap JOIN THE RANKING again.

Without the game

Send an e-mail to dnhfungames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my BoxSorter data" and your nickname (as shown in the game's Settings). We delete your entries within 30 days and answer you when it is done.

What is deleted / what is kept

  • Deleted: nickname, country, scores, times, the list of your achievements, the records of the promo codes you redeemed and the anonymous ranking ID.
  • Kept by us: nothing.
  • Your game progress (levels, coins, settings) is saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in the phone's settings, deletes it.
